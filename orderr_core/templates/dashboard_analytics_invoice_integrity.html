🚨 Invoice integrity
Every Vasy sales sync writes to a permanent history — deletions no longer disappear silently. This lists invoices that were present in an earlier sync but are missing from the latest one (i.e. deleted in Vasy after the fact). For each, we check whether that customer has a recorded receipt of roughly matching size anywhere between the invoice's date and a few days after it disappeared. No matching receipt doesn't prove fraud by itself — but it means nothing in the ledger explains where that invoice's amount went, so it's worth asking the customer or checking the Vasy audit log for who deleted it.
